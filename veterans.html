Get paid to care for your veteran

Who qualifies.

If you're already caring for a veteran in your family, here's what's needed. Call us and we'll confirm your situation.

The veteran

  • Is enrolled in VA health care (we can help them apply)
  • Needs help with everyday activities at home
  • Gets a VA referral for Homemaker and Home Health Aide care
  • Lives in an area we serve in southwest Missouri

You, the family caregiver

  • 18 or older
  • No paid caregiving experience needed for your own family member
  • Pass our background checks before you start
  • Complete our orientation, from home on your phone or computer
  • Become a W-2 employee, paid weekly

We hold your hand

The paperwork, done with you.

VA forms can feel like a lot. You don't have to figure them out alone, and you don't need a computer.

  1. We fill it out together, over the phone. We go through each form with you, one question at a time.
  2. We send it in for you. We can fax or send the completed forms for you, or mail you a copy that's ready to sign.
  3. We help with the VA referral. We help you ask the veteran's VA primary care team for a Homemaker and Home Health Aide referral to Caring Companions.
  4. We follow up. We keep in touch until care is set up, so nothing gets stuck.

Common questions

Questions veterans' families ask.

Does the veteran pay anything?

Once the VA authorizes Homemaker and Home Health Aide care, the VA pays Caring Companions directly, so you don't pay us for VA-authorized care. The VA has its own copay rules for some veterans, so ask the VA care team if you're unsure.

Can I get paid to care for my own veteran?

Yes. If your family member is a veteran with a VA Homemaker and Home Health Aide authorization, Caring Companions can hire you as their caregiver. You'd be our employee: 18 or older, background checks before you start, and our orientation. No paid caregiving experience is needed when you're caring for your own family member.

What paperwork is needed, and will you help?

Yes, we help from start to finish. We fill out the forms with you over the phone, then fax or send them in for you, or mail you a copy ready to sign. If the veteran isn't enrolled in VA health care yet, that starts with the VA's application (VA Form 10-10EZ), and we can do that with you too.

How do we get started?

Fill out the short form on this page or call (417) 234-8494. We'll explain the steps, help with the paperwork, and help you ask the veteran's VA primary care team for a Homemaker and Home Health Aide referral to Caring Companions.

Can the caregiver drive the veteran to appointments?

Not under the VA program. Caregivers can't transport VA Community Care clients in any vehicle, so the visits are for help at home.

What if the veteran also gets Aid & Attendance?

That's a separate VA pension benefit paid to the veteran or surviving spouse. Families often use it for care the VA doesn't authorize. Our VA benefits guide explains how it works.

Where do you serve?

Our home counties are Greene, Christian, Webster and Polk. We also serve Taney, Lawrence, Dallas and Stone counties when a qualified caregiver is nearby. See where we serve.
