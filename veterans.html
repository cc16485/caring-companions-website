Overview

About the VA program.

The VA's Homemaker and Home Health Aide benefit pays for help at home for veterans enrolled in VA health care. Caring Companions is in network with the VA Community Care Network, so with a referral from the veteran's VA doctor, the VA pays us for the authorized hours. Many veterans would rather not have a stranger in the house, and they don't have to: your veteran chooses who cares for them.

Someone you already knowA family member or friend we hire

The son, daughter, other family member or friend who's already helping can become the veteran's paid caregiver. We hire them, run the background checks, and train them with our orientation.

Someone you meet firstA caregiver we match

No one in the family can do it? We match a caregiver by skills, personality and schedule, and you meet them before the first visit.

Eligibility

Eligibility requirements.

Both the veteran and the caregiver need to meet a few requirements. Call us and we'll confirm your situation.

The veteran

  • Is enrolled in VA health care (we can help them apply)
  • Needs help with everyday activities at home
  • Gets a VA referral for Homemaker and Home Health Aide care
  • Lives in an area we serve in southwest Missouri

The caregiver (family member or friend)

  • 18 or older
  • No paid caregiving experience needed to care for a veteran you know
  • Passes our background checks before starting
  • Completes our orientation, from home on a phone or computer
  • Becomes a W-2 employee, paid weekly

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Can family members or friends get paid to care for a veteran?

Yes. If your family member or friend is a veteran with a VA Homemaker and Home Health Aide authorization, Caring Companions can hire you as their caregiver. You'd be our employee: 18 or older, background checks before you start, and our orientation. No paid caregiving experience is needed to care for a veteran you know.

Does the veteran need to be elderly?

No. Any veteran enrolled in VA health care who needs help with everyday activities at home may qualify. The VA decides what care to authorize.

Does the veteran pay anything?

Once the VA authorizes Homemaker and Home Health Aide care, the VA pays Caring Companions directly, so you don't pay us for VA-authorized care. The VA has its own copay rules for some veterans, so ask the VA care team if you're unsure.

What paperwork is needed, and will you help?

Yes, we help from start to finish. We fill out the forms with you over the phone, then fax or send them in for you, or mail you a copy ready to sign. If the veteran isn't enrolled in VA health care yet, that starts with the VA's application (VA Form 10-10EZ), and we can do that with you too.

How often is the caregiver paid?

Every week. You're a W-2 Caring Companions employee, paid weekly for the authorized hours you work.

Can the caregiver drive the veteran to appointments?

Not under the VA program. Caregivers can't transport VA Community Care clients in any vehicle, so the visits are for help at home.

What if the veteran also gets Aid & Attendance?

That's a separate VA pension benefit paid to the veteran or surviving spouse. Families often use it for care the VA doesn't authorize. Our VA benefits guide explains how it works.

Where do you serve?

Our home counties are Greene, Christian, Webster and Polk. We also serve Taney, Lawrence, Dallas and Stone counties when a qualified caregiver is nearby. See where we serve.
