You don’t have to wait until more help is needed.

Mom is doing well at home. Dad still manages his day. But you wish someone could stop by regularly, lend a hand, and spend a little time with them.

That’s why we created Early Support Visits.

A caregiver from our team helps with everyday things while getting to know your loved one. With their permission, we’ll let you know how each visit went.

And from the first visit, your family is established with Caring Companions. You know us, we know your loved one, and there is a whole team of wonderful caregivers behind every visit. So when an extra visit would help, or more help is needed someday, you can simply ask.

For families: you’ll hear how the visit went

If your mom or dad would like, our office sends you a short update after each visit:

  • What we helped with
  • How the visit went
  • Anything we noticed that seemed worth mentioning

Over time, our caregivers get to know your loved one: what makes them laugh, how they take their coffee, what a usual day looks like.

Updates are a caregiver’s everyday observations. They are not a medical check, and they can’t promise to catch every change.

What a visit looks like

Fresh sheets on the bed. A pot of soup on the stove. A trip to the store. And someone good to talk to while it all gets done.

Yes, our caregivers really clean. A visit can include:

  • Dishes, the kitchen and the counters
  • Sweeping, vacuuming and mopping
  • Cleaning the bathroom
  • Laundry and changing the bed
  • Tidying and a little organizing
  • Making a meal, with extra for later
  • An errand or a ride

What fits in a visit

Each visit is four hours. You and your caregiver decide together what matters most that day, and the good company happens right alongside the work.

These visits keep a home comfortable from one visit to the next. They aren’t meant for a top-to-bottom deep clean or a big cleaning project. If the house needs that first, we’re glad to suggest a cleaning company.

Early Support Visits or regular home care?

Early Support Visits are for people who manage their own day. They are not a replacement for regular care when someone needs more.

Early Support Visits

  • Doing well at home and managing their own day.
  • Would enjoy a hand with the house and some good company.
  • A visit now and then is plenty.

Regular home care

  • Help with bathing, dressing or getting around.

Questions people ask

Why start visits if my parent doesn’t need home care yet?

Because this is the easy time to start. Your parent is well enough to enjoy the visits, to say how they like things done, and to get comfortable with someone new at their own pace.

They get a hand with the things that are getting a little harder, and some good company. You get to know that someone who knows them is stopping by, and you hear how it went.

And if they ever do need more help, nobody is starting from scratch with strangers.

Do your caregivers really clean?

Yes. Kitchens, floors, bathrooms, laundry and the bed. What a visit isn’t meant for is the big once-a-year jobs, like carpets, outside windows or the garage. For those, we’re glad to suggest a cleaning company.

Will it be the same caregiver each time?

We do our best to send someone your loved one knows. Sometimes it will be another member of our team, and we always tell them who is coming.

How do we pick the day?

We’ll work together to find a regular visit day and time that fits your schedule and our caregiver availability.

What if we need to move or skip a visit?

With at least 24 hours’ notice, we’ll move it at no charge. With less than 24 hours’ notice, the visit is billed at 2 hours.

Can we take a break?

Yes. Visits can be paused for up to 60 days. Just let the office know.

What is the $99 for?

It is a one-time assessment and setup fee. Before visits begin, we come to the home, get to know your loved one, and write a personal care plan for the caregiver. It is separate from the four-hour visits and is billed with the first visit. Your first phone conversation with us is free.

Where do you offer it?

In Springfield for now. If the home is somewhere else nearby, tell us on the form and we will let you know what we can offer.

Can we add an extra visit?

Yes, just ask. Because your loved one is already a Caring Companions client, we know them and their home, and we will do our best to arrange an extra visit with a caregiver from our team. Extra visits depend on caregiver availability.

What if they need more help later?

Then we talk about it together. If your loved one starts to need help more often, or hands-on help with things like bathing or dressing, regular home care is the right fit, and we can move them over without starting from the beginning. Early Support Visits aren’t a substitute for that kind of care. Plenty of people will never need more, and they are welcome to stay with Early Support Visits for as long as it fits.
