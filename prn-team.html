Strict once you're on call

Our On-Call Expectations

You read and sign these before your first on-call shift.

  1. On call comes in two shifts: daytime, 7am to 7pm, and overnight, 7pm to 7am.
  2. Keep your phone on and with you. Answer the office, or call back, within 15 minutes.
  3. Be ready to start a shift within 1 hour.
  4. If the office calls you about an open shift during your on-call time, you cover it.
  5. If you don't answer the office within 15 minutes, or you don't cover the shift, you won't be paid for that on-call shift, and you won't be able to sign up for on call again.
  6. If something comes up, call the office at least 24 hours before your on-call shift starts. To stop an every-week on-call shift, call the office at least 24 hours before the next one starts.

Want more hours later?

PRN can lead to a regular schedule, if you want one.

It is never required. If you'd like to move to a regular schedule with an ongoing client, the ongoing rate is $16 an hour for caregivers and $18 an hour for CNAs.

Questions we hear

What if I say no to a shift?

That's fine. No pressure to accept, and no guilt for declining. You'll keep hearing from us when an opportunity may fit.

How will I hear about shifts?

By text, when we have an opportunity that may fit the days and times you told us you're generally available.

Is there a minimum number of shifts?

No. There may be times when you can help often and times when you can't help at all.

Which team should I apply for?

If you hold a current CNA certification, apply for the PRN CNA Team. If not, apply for the PRN Caregiver Team. You need at least a year of paid caregiving experience for either one.

Can the PRN Caregiver Team be on call?

To start, on-call is for the PRN CNA Team. If you earn your CNA, you can join the PRN CNA Team and choose on-call times.

How does hiring work?

Apply online in about 2 minutes, then a 20-minute in-person interview at our Springfield office. After that you can do the rest from home: paperwork online, a 15-minute Orientation Video Call, and paid orientation and training. See our recruitment process.
