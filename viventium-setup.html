Part one

Create your Viventium account

Same order as the first video, in case you would rather read them or need to check one thing later.

1

Find the email

Look in your email for a message with the subject line Register for Viventium ESS, and select Get Started. If you do not see it, check your spam folder.

2

Verify your Social Security number

Enter your nine-digit Social Security number, then select Verify SSN.

3

Verify your birthdate

Enter your date of birth exactly as we have it on file, then select Verify Birthdate. If it will not accept it, call the office and we will check what we have.

4

Enter your email address

This is what you will log in with from now on, so use an address you actually check. Select Next.

5

Create your password

Use at least eight characters, and mix in letters and numbers. Type it twice, then select Save Password.

6

Activate your account

One more email arrives, this one says You're Almost There. Select Activate Your Account, and your registration is complete.

7

Install the Viventium app

Search for Viventium in Google Play on an Android phone, or the App Store on an iPhone, and install it. Look for the purple icon with the V.

The app is not optional

Your pay stubs and your W-2 live in the Viventium app. We do not hand out printed pay stubs, so if you do not install it, you will not have a way to see what you were paid.

Part two

Direct deposit and tax withholding

You can do all of this in the app, or online at hcm.viventium.com/apps/auth/ess/login.

1

Sign in

Use the email address and the password you just created, and select Sign In.

2

Open My Pay

Both your direct deposits and your tax withholdings live on this page.

3

Change an account you already have

Select Update, edit the details, and select Update again.

If you have more than one account, a second screen lets you set how much goes to each one.

4

Add a new account

Select Add Direct Deposits. Enter the account type, the routing number, and the account number twice, then click Next.

On the next screen, enter the amount or the percentage that should go to that account, and click Create.

5

Update your tax withholding

Select Update next to the withholding you want to change, edit the information, such as your filing status, and select Update in the bottom-right corner.

6

Wait for approval

Every change comes to the office to be approved. You will see a banner that says Changes waiting for approval until it is. Click View Requests, or the clock icon, to check on it.

Why you get a text message

When you change where your pay is deposited, Viventium sends you a verification code by text. That is on purpose, so that nobody but you can move your paycheck to a different account.

If a code never arrives, or a change is still sitting there pending, call the office at 417-234-8494 and we will take a look.

Part two · Opening

Now let's set up where your money goes. You can do this in the app, or online at hcm.viventium.com/apps/auth/ess/login.

Sign in

Sign in with the email and password you just created, and select Sign In.

My Pay

Open My Pay. Both your direct deposits and your tax withholdings live on this page.

Change an account

To change an account you already have, select Update, edit the details, and select Update again. If you have more than one account, a second screen lets you set how much goes to each one.

Add an account

To add a new account, select Add Direct Deposits. Enter the account type, the routing number, and the account number twice, then click Next. On the next screen enter the amount or percentage for that account, and click Create.

Approval

After you save, the change comes to the office for approval. You'll see a banner that says Changes waiting for approval until it's approved, click View Requests, or the clock icon, to check on it. You'll also get a verification code by text, so nobody but you can change where your pay lands.

Taxes

Tax withholding works the same way. Select Update next to the withholding you want to change, edit the information, like your filing status, and select Update in the bottom-right corner. That one goes for approval too.

Part two closing

That's everything. If a code never shows up, or a change is still sitting there pending, call the office at 417-234-8494 and we'll take a look.
