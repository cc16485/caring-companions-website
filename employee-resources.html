Shifts and pay

How your week works

Your schedule lives in AxisCare

AxisCare is your official schedule. Check it every day, and keep your availability up to date with the office so we can offer you the hours you want.

How to clock in and out

Payday is every Friday

Our work week runs Monday through Sunday, and you are paid that Friday. Your pay stubs and your W-2 are in the Viventium app.

Set up Viventium

Overtime

Hours past 40 in a work week are paid at time and a half. Check with your coordinator before you pick up hours that would put you over 40.

Your training

Your coordinator sends your courses by text or email. Everyone starts with our 2-hour Agency Orientation. Courses like Alzheimer’s and dementia care are matched to the clients you care for.

When plans change

Can’t make a shift, or something happens

Office line, day or night 417-234-8494

The office is open Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. After hours, the same number reaches our after-hours coordinator.

CallText

If a client falls

  1. Do not lift them or help them up, even if they ask.
  2. Stay with them. Check for injury and keep them comfortable and warm on the floor.
  3. Call 911 if they are hurt or you are not sure.
  4. Call the office every time. Every fall gets an incident report.

Calling off

  • Call the office at least 4 hours before your shift.
  • For a shift that starts 7 to 9 AM, call by 5 PM the day before.
  • Never leave a client until the office says it is OK.

Forgot to clock in or out

Call or text the office right away with your real start and end times. We check with the client and fix it for you.

Running late

Call the office as soon as you know, so we can let your client know you are on the way.

Friends & Family Referral Program

Help us grow, and get a bonus for it

You know people who need help at home, and people who would make great caregivers. Share your own personal link with them. When they start with us, you get a bonus on your paycheck.

Your link is yours alone, so every referral is tied to you. You don’t have to sell anything. Just share it, and we take it from there.

$100Refer a familyWhen a family you refer starts private-pay care with us and completes 20 hours, and payment for those hours is received.
$50 + $50Refer a caregiver$50 after the new caregiver’s first completed shift. Another $50 once they have been with us 60 days and worked 10 shifts, while you are both still on the team.

We send your link

We text and email you your own personal link and QR code. It also comes in our notes to you.

Share it your way

Text it, email it, post it, or show the QR code on your phone. Your Refer a Friend page has a Copy link button.

They use your link

Families fill out the short form or call us. Caregivers apply. Ask them to use your link so the bonus comes to you.

Paid on your paycheck

We track it for you. Once it is earned and approved, the bonus comes on your regular payday.

Can’t find your link? Call or text the office at 417-234-8494 and we will send it again. Family referrals count for private-pay care. If you refer someone to our CDS program, we can’t pay a reward, since CDS is a Medicaid program.
