Mobility Assistance

Part of Personal Care · $32 an hour

A steady hand, so they can keep moving.

Falls usually happen during ordinary moves: getting out of bed, standing up from the toilet, turning in the kitchen. A caregiver gives a steady hand at those moments and helps your loved one keep walking, because staying active is what keeps them strong.

Call (417) 234-8494 Book a free in-home assessment

Not ready to talk yet? Ask Cara or fill out our Care Questionnaire (about 8 minutes).

Why it matters

Falls are common, serious and often preventable.

The CDC is clear that falls are not a normal part of aging. Steady support, a safer home and staying active all lower the risk.

1 in 4adults 65 and older fall each year.
#1cause of injury for adults 65 and older is a fall.
Mosthip fractures in older adults are caused by falls.

Sources: CDC, older adult falls; CDC MMWR, falls and hip fractures.

What's included

Help with every kind of move.

✓  Walking with them, inside and out

✓  Getting in and out of bed

✓  Bed to chair and chair to toilet

✓  In and out of the car

✓  Standing up and sitting down safely

✓  Using a cane, walker or wheelchair

✓  Gentle movement the therapist suggested

✓  Clearing trip hazards as we go

Mobility help is part of Personal Care, so it comes with everything in Wellness Care too: company, meals, housekeeping and outings. Caregivers can follow exercises a physical therapist has given, but they don't provide therapy.

How we do it

Safe, but never smothering.

Keep them moving

Doing too much for someone weakens them. We help them walk and stand on their own as much as they safely can.

Safe techniques

Caregivers help with standing, turning and transfers the safe way, using the equipment your loved one already has.

Out of the house

A wheelchair or walker doesn't have to mean staying in. We help with the car and go along on outings.

Signs it may be time

You might be noticing:

Holding onto furniture or walls to get around

A fall, or a near miss, in the last few months

Trouble getting up from a chair or the toilet

Shuffling, or afraid to walk without help

Bruises they can't explain

Skipping outings because getting there is hard

If you've noticed any of these, a free in-home assessment is a good next step. We'll look at the home, how they get around, and where a steady hand would help most.
