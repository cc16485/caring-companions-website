What is Overnight Care?

A caregiver comes to the home in the evening and stays through the night, typically an 8–12 hour shift from evening to morning, so someone is awake and ready to help with bathroom trips, wandering, medication reminders, or anything else that happens after dark. It sits between Hourly Care and full 24-Hour Care, ideal when nighttime safety is the main concern but round-the-clock daytime supervision isn't yet needed.

Who it's for

✓  Families most worried about nighttime falls or wandering

✓  Those who manage daytime care themselves but need overnight relief

✓  People recently discharged who need overnight monitoring temporarily

✓  Anyone comparing Overnight against 24-Hour Care
