Why professionals refer to us

Caregivers are our employees

Every caregiver is screened, background-checked, insured, and bonded, W-2 employees, not contractors. We carry the liability, taxes, and backup coverage.

Fast, reliable starts

We can often assess and begin care the same or next day, including nights and weekends, so your discharge plan doesn't stall.

Care around the clock

We staff overnight and 24-hour care, and we cover call-offs so families are never left without help.

We help unlock funding

At no cost to the family, we help apply for VA Aid & Attendance and the Medicare GUIDE dementia-respite benefit (up to 20 four-hour respite visits a year).

We keep you in the loop

With the family's consent, you get confirmation that care started and a point of contact for updates, so you can close the loop on your patient with confidence.

Broad coverage

We serve Springfield and Greene, Christian, Webster, and Polk counties, plus Taney, Lawrence, Dallas, and Stone counties when a caregiver is nearby. Call and we'll confirm.

How to refer, it takes 2 minutes

1

Reach out. Call (417) 234-8494, fax the referral to (417) 719-7870, or submit it online at our Referral Partner Portal. Share what you can; we'll gather the rest.

2

We take it from here. We contact the family, complete a free in-home assessment, build the care plan, and check for any funding they may qualify for.

3

Care begins, you're updated. Once the assessment and care plan are done and a caregiver is matched, care starts, sometimes the same day or next day, and we confirm back to you when it does.
