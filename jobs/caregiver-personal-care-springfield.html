Now hiring · Springfield, MO

Caregiver/CNA Bed-Bound Care

Personal care and transfers, 7 days a week. Pick morning (8am to 2pm) or evening (4pm to 10pm) shifts.

Part time Springfield, MO $17.00–$18.00/hr
Apply now →About the job

Caring Companions is building a small, steady team of caregivers for a client in Springfield who needs help with personal care and transfers.

Care is needed every day, in two shifts:
Mornings, 9am to 2pm
Evenings, 4pm to 9pm

That is 7 days a week, so we are hiring several caregivers. Pick the shifts and days that work for you, mornings, evenings or some of each, and we will build a schedule the whole team can count on.

You would be helping with bathing, dressing, toileting and safe transfers, along with meals, light housekeeping and good company. We train you on safe transfer technique before you start, and experience with transfers or a CNA certification is a plus.

This is W-2 employment, not contract work. You are paid weekly, overtime is paid over 40 hours, and you complete paid orientation and training from home before you start.

What you would be doing

  • Follow the care plan and complete the care tasks assigned to you
  • Provide personal care: bathing, dressing, grooming, oral hygiene, toileting and incontinence care
  • Help with safe transfers and mobility, using proper body mechanics and equipment such as gait belts
  • Give medication reminders (not medication administration)
  • Prepare meals and keep the home clean and safe, including light housekeeping and laundry
  • Offer companionship and conversation
  • Notice and promptly report any change in the client's condition, safety or surroundings
  • Clock in and out on AxisCare and finish your notes before the end of each shift
  • Hand off clearly to the next caregiver so nothing is missed between shifts

What we are looking for

  • Have at least one year of paid caregiving experience, or a current CNA, CMT, LPN or RN license
  • Be at least 21 years old
  • Pass all required background screenings
  • Have reliable transportation and a working phone
  • Complete orientation, onboarding, dementia and Alzheimer's training, and annual in-service training
  • Be able to stand, walk, bend and assist with transfers, and to safely lift up to 50 pounds where authorized, using proper technique and equipment such as gait belts
  • Experience with transfers or a CNA certification is a plus

What we offer

  • W-2 employment, not a contractor arrangement
  • Paid weekly
  • Overtime paid for hours over 40 in a workweek
  • Paid orientation and training from home
  • Choose your shifts: mornings, evenings, or both

How hiring works

After your interview, you can do the rest from home.

  1. Apply online. About 2 minutes, no uploads. Pick your interview time at the end.
  2. Interview. A short interview, about 20 minutes, so we can get to know you.
  3. Offer and paperwork. Fill out your new-hire paperwork online. We run your background checks and call your references, at no cost to you.
  4. Orientation & training. Paid, from home on your phone or computer, in pieces if you like.

Interested?

The application takes about 2 minutes. No uploads required. One short interview, then the rest from home.
