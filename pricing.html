Transparent Pricing

Trusted care. Clear pricing. No surprises.

Finding the right care for someone you love is a big decision. Understanding the cost shouldn't make it harder.

At Caring Companions, we believe families deserve honest, upfront pricing without the pressure of a sales call. That's why we share our rates right here, so you can explore your options, plan with confidence, and focus on what matters most: finding the right care for your loved one.

A helping hand and a friendly face.

Perfect for someone who is mostly independent but could use companionship, help around the house, or a little extra support throughout the day.

Extra support for more complex care needs.

For individuals living with advanced dementia, significant mobility challenges, or other conditions requiring closer attention and more experienced caregivers.

Care that fits your life, not the other way around. Choose a schedule that works for your family, with a minimum of just 4 hours per visit and 8 hours per week. Every client receives a personalized care plan, and our team works to match your loved one with the right caregiver.

More than a caregiver. A team you can count on.

When you choose Caring Companions, you're getting more than help around the house. You're getting a locally owned team that takes the time to know your loved one, understands your family's concerns, and works to make life a little easier.

We're not a franchise, and when you call our office, you're reaching people right here in your community. We believe great care starts with genuine relationships, clear communication, and treating every family like our own.

What each level includes

Wellness Care $30/hour

Helping your loved one stay independent, connected, and comfortable at home.

✓ Friendly companionship and meaningful conversation
✓ Meal planning and preparation
✓ Light housekeeping and laundry
✓ Grocery shopping, errands, and transportation
✓ Medication reminders and daily routine support
✓ Encouragement to stay active and engaged
✓ Help with phones, tablets and video calls
✓ Pet care, plants, mail and simple paperwork
✓ Hobbies, photo albums and memory books

Designed for individuals who do not require hands-on personal care.

Personal Care $32/hour

Gentle, respectful assistance with everyday activities, while encouraging independence and preserving dignity.

Everything in Wellness Care, plus:

✓ Bathing, showering, and personal hygiene

✓ Assistance with walking and mobility
✓ Help with safe transfers and positioning
✓ Assistance with meals and eating

Advanced Care $34/hour

Thoughtful, attentive care for loved ones who need a higher level of daily support.

Everything in Personal Care, plus support for:

✓ Advanced Alzheimer’s disease and other dementias
✓ Increased supervision due to confusion or wandering
✓ Significant mobility limitations
✓ Parkinson’s disease and stroke-related challenges
✓ More involved personal care and daily routines
✓ Families navigating changing care needs

Caregivers are thoughtfully matched to your loved one's needs. Advanced Care is still non-medical care, not skilled nursing.
