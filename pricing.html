What each level includes

Wellness Care $30/hour

Helping your loved one stay independent, connected, and comfortable at home.

✓ Friendly companionship and meaningful conversation
✓ Meal planning and preparation
✓ Light housekeeping and laundry
✓ Grocery shopping, errands, and transportation
✓ Medication reminders and daily routine support
✓ Encouragement to stay active and engaged
✓ Help with phones, tablets and video calls
✓ Pet care, plants, mail and simple paperwork
✓ Hobbies, photo albums and memory books

Designed for individuals who do not require hands-on personal care.

Personal Care $32/hour

Everything in Wellness Care, plus:

✓ Bathing, showering, and personal hygiene

✓ Assistance with meals and eating

Advanced Care $34/hour

Thoughtful, attentive care for loved ones who need a higher level of daily support.

Everything in Personal Care, plus support for:

✓ Advanced Alzheimer’s disease and other dementias
✓ Increased supervision due to confusion or wandering
✓ Significant mobility limitations
✓ Parkinson’s disease and stroke-related challenges
✓ More involved personal care and daily routines
✓ Families navigating changing care needs

Caregivers are thoughtfully matched to your loved one's needs. Advanced Care is still non-medical care, not skilled nursing.
