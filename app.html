Coming soon, for Caring Companions clients

Book care in about a minute.

The Caring Companions app is for our clients. Care starts with a free living room meeting at home, where we get to know your loved one and put a care plan together with you. After that, the app puts more control in your hands: book Wellness Care visits when you need them, right from your phone, with no weekly minimum.

Get early access

How it will work

Meet first, then book when you need us.

  1. A living room meeting

    We come to the home, free, meet your loved one and learn their routines, what they enjoy and what's gotten harder.

  2. A care plan together

    We write down the help they need and the caregivers who fit, so every visit starts from the same page.

  3. Book in the app

    Add Wellness Care visits when you need them, with no weekly minimum. Pick a date and a set time or a flexible window, any day from 7am to 6pm, for 2 hours or more. No weekly minimum and no extra charge for short visits. Say who you'd like to come, and see the cost before you book.

The app is free any month you have a visit. Months without a visit are $15.

What you can book in the app

The app books Wellness Care (Level 1, $30 an hour): company and conversation, meal preparation, light housekeeping and laundry, groceries and errands, rides, medication reminders, outings, help with phones and video calls, pets and plants, mail, and hobbies.

Hands-on help like bathing, dressing or transfers is Personal Care (Level 2), and our care team schedules it with you.

Good to know

The app is a great way to reach our care team and ask for a visit whenever you need one. Visits booked in the app depend on which caregivers are free at that time, so we can't promise a caregiver will be available for every request.

See it as one more option for help. If your loved one needs care they can count on at set times, a regular schedule in their care plan is the way to make sure of it.
