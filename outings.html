How outings work

Simple, safe and planned with you.

  • Outings happen during your loved one's scheduled visits, weekdays or weekends.
  • You approve each place before we go, and we write favorites into their care plan.
  • The caregiver drives and stays with them the whole time.
  • Admission, meals and class fees are paid by the family.
  • On hot or icy days, we swap in an indoor plan.
