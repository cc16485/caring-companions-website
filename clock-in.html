Good to know

Four things that keep your pay right

Location on

Choose Allow While Using App. AxisCare checks you are at your client’s home when you clock in and out.

Every single visit

Clock in when you walk in the door. Clock out before you leave. That is how your time is proven and paid.

Forgot one?

Call or text the office right away at 417-234-8494 with your real start and end times. We check with the client and fix it.

Only real time

Never clock in for time you were not there. That can end your job with us.

If something goes wrong

Quick fixes

The activation code never came

  1. Use the same email address we have on file for you.
  2. Check your spam or junk folder for a message from AxisCare.
  3. Still nothing? Call 417-234-8494. We can look up your account.

It will not clock me in

  1. Make sure location is on for AxisCare in your phone’s settings.
  2. Step near a window or outside the front door, wait a minute, and try again.
  3. Still stuck? Call or text 417-234-8494, day or night, and tell us the time you started.
