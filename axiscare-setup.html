The steps, in writing

Same order as the video, in case you would rather read them or need to check one thing later.

1

Download the app

Open the App Store on an iPhone or the Google Play Store on an Android phone and search for AxisCare Mobile, one word. Tap Get. Look for the blue and yellow icon.

2

Enter our server number

Open the app. The first thing it asks for is your agency's server number. Ours is 16485. Type it in and tap Next.

3

Activate your account

Choose Activate Account and enter the email address you gave us. It has to be the same email we have on file, or the code will not reach you. Then tap Send activation code.

4

Check your email

Open the message from AxisCare, then type that code into the app. You may also be asked to confirm your date of birth or ZIP code.

5

Create your password

Type it once, then again to confirm. Pick something you will remember, because you will use it every shift.

6

Log in

Enter your username and the password you just created. Your Caring Companions schedule and upcoming visits appear.

7

Allow location access

When your phone asks, tap Allow While Using App. AxisCare uses your location to confirm you are at the client's home when you clock in and out. If you do not allow it, your clock-ins may not go through.

Clock in and out every single visit

The state requires that visits are clocked in and out correctly at least 90% of the time. This is not a Caring Companions rule, it is how the visit gets billed and proven.

If you do forget to clock out, fill out the EVV Correction Form with your name, the visit, the corrected times, the reason, and your signature, and submit it by Sunday midnight of that pay period. Missing clock-ins, or skipping this form when you forget, can lead to discipline, up to termination.

Opening

Hi, this is Samantha with Caring Companions. In this short video I'll show you how to download and log into the AxisCare app.

Download the app

Start by opening the App Store on an iPhone, or the Google Play Store on an Android phone. Search for AxisCare Mobile, that's AxisCare, one word, and tap Get to download it. Look for the blue and yellow icon.

Enter our server number

Now open the app. The first thing AxisCare asks for is your agency's server number. For Caring Companions, that number is one, six, four, eight, five. Type it in, then tap Next.

Activate your account

If this is your first time using AxisCare, choose Activate Account. Enter the email address you gave to Caring Companions. It has to be the same email we have on file for you, or the code won't come through. Then tap Send activation code.

Check your email

Open your email and look for the message from AxisCare. It contains your activation code. Go back to the app and type that code in. AxisCare may also ask you to confirm something like your date of birth or your ZIP code.

Create your password

Next, create your password. Type it once, then type it again to confirm. Pick something you'll remember, because you'll use this password every shift from now on.

Log in

Now you're ready to log in. Enter your AxisCare username and the password you just created. Once you're in, you'll see your Caring Companions schedule and your upcoming visits.

Allow location access

This next part is very important. When your phone asks whether AxisCare can use your location, tap Allow While Using App. AxisCare uses your phone's location to confirm you're at the client's home when you clock in and clock out. If you don't allow it, your clock-ins may not go through.

EVV and the correction form

One more thing, and please listen closely. The state requires that you clock in and out correctly at least ninety percent of the time. If you do forget to clock out, you must fill out our EVV Correction Form at sc.mo-care.com slash evv-correction-form. Enter your name, the visit, the corrected times, the reason, and your signature, and submit it by Sunday midnight of that pay period. Missing clock-ins, or skipping this form when you forget, can lead to discipline, up to termination.

Closing
