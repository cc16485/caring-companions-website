How getting started works

Choosing home care is a big decision. Here's what happens, from the first phone call to the first visit.

  1. We talk on the phone

    Call us, or ask for a call above. A care coordinator listens, answers your questions about care and cost, and there's no pressure to decide anything.

  2. A free visit at home

    We come to the house for a free in-home assessment, meet your loved one, and build the care plan together. No obligation, and you get everything in writing. When you’re ready, we go over and sign your Client Care Agreement together before care starts. Read a sample agreement (PDF)

  3. Meet your caregiver first

    We match a caregiver by skills, personality and schedule, then send you their profile with a photo before the first visit.

  4. Care begins, and we stay close

    We call a few days after the first visit to see how it's going, and a real person answers our office line, day or night.

Questions families ask

How much does care cost?

$30, $32 or $34 an hour, depending on the level of care: Wellness Care, Personal Care or Advanced Care. We'll recommend the right level together at the visit. Your rate is written in your agreement, and we give at least 4 weeks' written notice before any change.

Is there a deposit or a long-term contract?

No deposit and no long-term contract. You're billed weekly, after the visits have happened, from your caregiver's actual clock-in and clock-out times. You can stop care when you need to.

Is there a minimum?

Our standard visit is at least 4 hours, with at least 8 hours a week. Need a shorter visit? Please ask. When a caregiver is available for a shorter shift, we will work to make it happen, with an extra $5 an hour for visits under 4 hours. Shorter visits depend on staffing, so we cannot promise them every time.

Is the assessment free? Do we have to decide that day?

Yes, it's free, and there's no obligation. We listen, answer your questions and leave you everything in writing. Take the time you need.

Will we have the same caregiver each visit?

That's our goal. A familiar face builds trust, and a caregiver who knows your loved one's routines is more likely to notice when something is off. We work to keep the same caregiver with you, or the same small group of caregivers when the schedule needs more than one person. Caregivers do get sick and take time off, so there will be days when someone fills in. A regular, familiar caregiver is what we work toward for every client.

Will we know who's coming?

Always. Before the first visit, we send your caregiver's profile with a friendly photo. Our goal is for that same caregiver to keep coming. If someone ever has to fill in, such as when your caregiver is sick, the family members you choose get a text with that caregiver's profile.

Do you do background checks?

Yes, on every caregiver and every office team member, before they start: federal, state and county background checks, a driving record check, and verification that they're allowed to work. The checks are repeated over time, not just done once.

Are caregivers your employees?

Always. Every caregiver is a Caring Companions employee, never a contractor. We handle their payroll and all their taxes, so you never become anyone's employer. Every caregiver also has at least a year of paid caregiving experience and finishes our orientation before their first visit with a client.
