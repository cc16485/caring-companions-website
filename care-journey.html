Your Care Journey

What actually happens after you call.

Choosing home care is one of the harder decisions a family makes, and not knowing what comes next makes it harder. Here is every step, in order, with the timing we hold ourselves to.

1
Whenever you're ready

You tell us what's going on

Call us, book online, or work through the care assessment first if you'd rather gather your thoughts before speaking to anyone. There is no script and no obligation, and you do not need to know what kind of care you want yet. Most families do not.

2
Within 24 hours

We reach out to schedule a free in-home consultation

A care coordinator contacts you within 24 hours to arrange a visit at a time that suits your family. The consultation happens in the home, because the home is half the picture: the stairs, the bathroom, the lighting, how far the bedroom is from the kitchen.

It is free, and it carries no obligation to book care.

3
At the consultation

We build a care plan around the needs and the budget

We write a plan for your situation, not a package off a shelf, covering what help is needed, when it is actually needed, and what it will cost. Our rates are posted publicly, so nothing about the number should surprise you.

If another way of paying fits better, we will say so, whether that is Medicaid, VA benefits, or long-term care insurance.

4
Before the first visit

We match a caregiver, and train them on the plan

We match on personality, experience, and care needs, not just who happens to be free. Every caregiver is trained on your loved one's care plan before they walk through the door, so they arrive already knowing the preferences and routines that matter.

  • Matched by personality, experience, and care needs
  • Trained on the care plan before the first visit
  • Preferences and routines honored from day one
  • Employees of ours, not contractors, and covered by our insurance
5
Often 24–48 hours after the plan

Care begins

Once the service plan is in place, care usually starts within 24 to 48 hours, depending on caregiver availability in your area. If your situation is urgent, say so on the first call and we will tell you honestly what we can do.

Who you'll be working with

Two people stand behind every care plan, so you are never left wondering who to call.

Your Care Coordinator

The person you talk to. They review your situation, run the free in-home consultation, write the care plan, and stay your point of contact afterwards, when needs change or you simply want to talk something through.

Your Staffing Coordinator

The person behind the schedule. They match the caregiver to your loved one, hold the calendar, and arrange coverage when your regular caregiver is away, so a gap in the rota never becomes your problem to solve.

And if the fit isn't right?

Then we change it. Matching is a judgment, and judgments are sometimes wrong, so we keep checking the fit rather than assuming it held. Tell us it isn't working and we will match someone else.

The same goes for the plan itself. Needs shift, and a plan written in March may be wrong by August. Yours is meant to be rewritten when that happens, not defended.

Where we do this

We serve Greene, Christian, Douglas, Phelps, Taney, and Webster counties. If you are just outside them, call anyway and we will tell you plainly whether we can help or point you to someone who can. See the full service area.
