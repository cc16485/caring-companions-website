How it works

  1. Share your link or QR code with a friend, neighbor or relative who could use help at home.
  2. They reach us through your link. They can fill out the short form or call us, and we take it from there.
  3. When they start private-pay care with us and complete 20 hours, and payment for those hours is received, you get $100: a credit on your family's Caring Companions invoice, or a bonus on your paycheck if you're one of our caregivers.

The small print

  • The person you refer must be new to Caring Companions, start private-pay care, and complete 20 hours of care, with payment for those hours received.
  • One reward per new client. If two people refer the same family, the first link they used counts.
  • Family members: the $100 goes on the invoice of the client you're connected to. Caregivers: a $100 bonus on your paycheck, taxed like other pay.
  • Credits can't be exchanged for cash.
  • We never share anything about the person you refer or their care with you.
