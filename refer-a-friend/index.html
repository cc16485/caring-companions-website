How it works

  1. Share your link or QR code with a friend, neighbor or relative who could use help at home.
  2. They reach us through your link. They can fill out the short form or call us, and we take it from there.
  3. When they start private-pay care with us and complete 20 hours, and payment for those hours is received, you get a thank-you: $200 off your family's Caring Companions invoice, or a $100 bonus on your paycheck if you're one of our caregivers.

Help us grow!

Who to keep an eye out for

You don't have to sell anything. If someone comes to mind, share your link and we'll take it from there.

Someone who could use a hand at home

  • An older neighbor, friend or relative who lives alone and is slowing down.
  • Someone just home from the hospital or rehab.
  • Someone who needs help with bathing, dressing, meals, laundry or getting to appointments.
  • Someone whose memory is changing, or who shouldn't be left alone for long.
  • A husband, wife, son or daughter who is worn out from doing all the caring themselves and needs a break.
  • Someone who is lonely and would brighten up with regular company.

This is the referral that earns your thank-you, when they start private-pay care with us.

Someone on Missouri Medicaid who needs help at home

They may be able to choose a family member or friend to be their paid caregiver through our Consumer Directed Services program. Point them to caringcds.com or have them call us.

Someone caring for a veteran

A family member or friend may be able to get paid to care for a veteran they already help. Details are on our veterans page.

We can't give a reward for Medicaid, VA or GUIDE referrals, because of the rules those programs follow. Please send them our way anyway: it may be exactly the help they need.

For our caregivers: someone who would be great at this work

  • Someone kind, patient and dependable, 21 or older, who would be new to Caring Companions.
  • A CNA or caregiver you've worked with and would trust with your own family.
  • Someone looking for steady hours, or for extra shifts on our PRN Team.

Share the same link. It also shows our open jobs. When someone you refer is hired, you get a $50 bonus on your paycheck after their first shift, and another $50 once they have been with us 60 days and worked 10 shifts, with both of you still on the team.

A few promises we all keep

  • Thank-yous for referring someone for care are for private-pay care only.
  • If you also work at a hospital, nursing home or another agency, never point that workplace's patients or residents to us. Friends, family, church and neighbors only.
  • Caregivers never bring up referrals during a visit in a client's home. If a family asks, it's fine to share your card.
  • Someone you refer for a job goes through the same interview, background checks and steps as everyone else.

The small print

  • The person you refer must be new to Caring Companions, start private-pay care, and complete 20 hours of care, with payment for those hours received.
  • One reward per new client. If two people refer the same family, the first link they used counts.
  • Family members: $200 comes off the invoice of the client you're connected to. Caregivers: a $100 bonus on your paycheck, taxed like other pay.
  • Caregivers who refer a new caregiver: $50 after the new hire's first shift and $50 at 60 days and 10 shifts, on your paycheck and taxed like other pay. One bonus per new hire; the first link they used counts. Family members' links are for referring someone for care.
  • Credits can't be exchanged for cash.
  • We never share anything about the person you refer or their care with you.
